Beyond the résumé

A little more about me.

I’m Anastasiia — a mechanical engineering student, researcher, volunteer, and first-generation student leader building a life and an engineering career in Colorado.

I’m originally from Russia and moved to the United States in 2021. Since then, I have earned an associate degree, transferred to the Colorado School of Mines, and continued building my path in mechanical engineering.

My application for U.S. citizenship is currently pending. Becoming a citizen will mark an important personal milestone in the life I have built here.

What matters to me

Engineering, community, and making difficult paths easier to navigate.

Moving between countries and education systems taught me to become comfortable with unfamiliar situations, ask better questions, and find a way forward even when the path is not immediately clear.

Those experiences influence both my engineering work and the way I participate in my community. I enjoy solving technical problems, but I also care about sharing information, supporting other students, and helping make professional opportunities more accessible.

Community and leadership

Supporting students and contributing beyond the classroom.

I enjoy creating resources, volunteering at professional events, and helping students navigate unfamiliar academic and professional environments.

Student leadership

Tri-Alpha Honor Society

I serve as Vice-President of the Tri-Alpha Honor Society chapter at the Colorado School of Mines. Tri-Alpha recognizes and supports first-generation college students while creating a stronger community around their experiences.

My work includes planning events, developing partnerships with other student organizations, coordinating outreach, and creating opportunities for students to connect, volunteer, and support one another.

I also designed and built the chapter’s website to provide one central place for events, membership information, partnerships, and chapter updates.
